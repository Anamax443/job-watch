JobWatch

Automated job-vacancy monitor · executive summary · 1 Sep 2026
IN PRODUCTION

What it is for

Every day it goes through the entire Czech labour market, picks out IT leadership roles, assesses them against one specific candidate profile, and sends what matters to Telegram, e-mail or Slack. A person does not read job ads — only the result, and decides what to do with it.

The system runs in the cloud with no server of its own, independent of any switched-on computer, and continuously since 14 Jun 2026.

How it works

1 · COLLECT
100 ads a day
Labour Office open data (whole CZ market), job portals and company career pages.
2 · FILTER
86 of them drop out
Fixed rules on field, occupation code and region. The program decides, not the AI.
3 · ASSESSMENT
AI assesses 14
The single place where artificial intelligence decides: how well a role fits the profile.
4 · OUTPUT
A message to a human
Whatever passes the threshold arrives within 24 hours. At most 10 messages a day, to avoid an avalanche.

Measured on 1 Sep 2026 against 23 real job ads from the production database, for which the correct answer had been determined by hand in advance. The measurement runs inside the deployed version, not in a lab, so it describes exactly what serves live traffic.

Why that number matters. Until 1 Sep 2026 the system ran without any quality measurement — it worked, but nobody could show how well. The measurement immediately revealed that the free language model missed half of the suitable ads, while the paid one found all of them. The difference between CZK 0 and 82 a month is therefore the difference between half the opportunities and all of them.

Where the AI stops

The AI decides one thing only How well a role matches the profile. Field, region, duplicates, threshold and sending are handled by fixed rules in the program — things that can be tested and repeated.
The AI has no access to irreversible steps It returns a number and a short justification. Whether a message goes out is decided by the program.
Ad text is treated as data Job ads are written by outsiders. The text is therefore inserted into the query delimited and marked as untrusted, so that an instruction hidden inside cannot influence the system.
Failure is visible When a run crashes or the platform kills it, a message is sent. Found nothing today and it crashed today must not look the same.
